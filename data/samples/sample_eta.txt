SAMPLE ELECTRONIC TRANSACTIONS ACT, 2019

Section 1. Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Electronic Transactions Act, 2019.

Section 4. Legal recognition of data messages.
Information shall not be denied legal effect, validity or enforceability
solely on the grounds that it is in the form of a data message.

Section 7. Legal recognition of electronic signatures.
Where the law requires a signature of a person, that requirement is met by
an electronic signature if the method used—
(a) identifies the person; and
(b) indicates the person's approval of the information communicated.

Section 12. Formation and validity of electronic contracts.
A contract shall not be denied validity or enforceability solely on the ground
that a data message was used in its formation.
